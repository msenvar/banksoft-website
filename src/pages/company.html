About Banksoft

Three decades of card payment software, built in Istanbul.

Since 1995, Banksoft has focused on one thing: software for card payment systems. Research and development, system integration, maintenance and support, delivered as turnkey solutions to banks.

Who we are

A specialist, not a generalist.

Banksoft provides software solutions through research and development and system integration services related to card payment systems, along with consultancy and project management for payment technology. Since our founding the professional team has grown steadily while service quality has kept improving, and banks throughout Türkiye and abroad rely on us for their payment software.

We have led some of the market's largest transitions from complex legacy hosts to open platforms, cutting operating cost while adding the features the market demanded. The result is infrastructure that delivers high performance, availability, quality and functionality without performance limitations.

Milestones

Milestones since 1995.

A selection from three decades of firsts in the Turkish payments market, with the year each one shipped.

1995

Banksoft founded

Established in Istanbul to build card payment software for banks.

2008

Deloitte EMEA Technology Fast 500

Ranked 230th fastest-growing technology company in EMEA with 749% growth.

2009

Europe's first EMV prepaid contactless debit card

Halkbank's Bank24 Jet card launched on Banksoft solutions.

2010

First Turkish participating organization in the PCI Security Standards Council

A seat at the table where card security standards are written; Deloitte Fast 50 Türkiye for the fifth time the same year.

2012

BKM dual-messaging migration in one night

Banksoft systems carried the Interbank Card Center's big-bang switch from single to dual messaging.

Contactless dual-interface cards for transit
