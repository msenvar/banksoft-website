Privacy policy

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the map on the contact page is loaded from OpenStreetMap; those providers receive your IP address when the assets load, under their own privacy policies.

When you send a message through the contact form, the details you enter (name, institution, work email, topic and message) are transmitted to Banksoft and used only to reply to your enquiry. They are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting business relationship, then deleted.

Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, requested page, time, browser type) for security and operational purposes and deletes them on a rolling basis.

Questions about privacy on this website: contact@banksoft.com.tr.
